PhotocatalysisPapers

About this index

PhotocatalysisPapers is an automatically updated index of photocatalysis research papers: water splitting, hydrogen evolution, CO2 photoreduction, pollutant degradation, photoredox and organic synthesis, semiconductor photocatalysts, Z-scheme systems, photoelectrochemistry and solar fuels, covering experimental, computational and data-driven studies alike.

How it works

Every day an automated pipeline queries open scholarly metadata APIs, removes duplicates, classifies each paper with a photocatalysis relevance model, assigns categories and a relevance score, and regenerates this site.

Data policy

This site stores metadata only: title, authors, journal, date, DOI and link, and for some papers the address of the publisher's table-of-contents graphic. No abstracts, no full text, no PDFs, no images. Every paper links to the original publisher page. To have a record corrected or removed, contact guruprakash29011999@gmail.com.

Abstracts

A paper's Abstract button asks OpenAlex for the abstract by the paper's DOI, and Crossref when OpenAlex has none. Your browser sends that request itself, only when you click, and the abstract is shown with its source. Nothing is stored, here or in your browser. Papers without a DOI, or whose abstract neither service has, show "No abstract available".

Table-of-contents graphics

Every day the index reads the RSS feeds of the ACS, Wiley and RSC journals that publish papers listed here, and keeps, for those papers, only the address of the table-of-contents graphic the feed links. The image is not copied: your browser loads it from the publisher's own server, which therefore sees that request, as it would on the journal's website. A paper gets a graphic only while it is in its journal's feed, so mostly recent papers have one. So far 268 papers here have one (Wiley 268). Each graphic remains the property of its publisher or authors, is credited under it, and links to the paper.

What your browser keeps

Marking a paper read or saved, the journals you follow and the time of your last visit are kept in your browser's local storage on this computer, for this index only. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.
